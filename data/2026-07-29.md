Abstract: Self-supervised representation learning from unlabeled channel state information (CSI) can reduce labeling and adaptation overhead in learning-based multiple-input multiple-output (MIMO) systems. This paper proposes beam-response contrastive learning (BRCL), a self-supervised CSI pretraining framework based on the transmit-side Gram matrix.
